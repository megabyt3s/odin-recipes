Lumpia Recipe

Description

Lumpia is made with vegetables or served fresh instead of fried. Lumpiang sariwa uses a soft wrapper and peanut sauce, lumpiang gulay is packed with vegetables, and turon is the sweet kind made with banana.

Ingredients

  • Ground pork
  • Onion and carrot
  • Garlic powder
  • Eggs
  • Parsley
  • Sesame oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Lumpia wrappers
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Mix the Filling
    2. - Put the ground pork, minced onion and carrot, garlic powder, parsley, sesame oil, salt, pepper, and the two eggs in a bowl.
    - Mix everything well with your hands.
  2. Wrap the Lumpia
    3. - Lay a wrapper flat and put about a tablespoon of filling near one edge.
    - Spread it into a thin line and fold the sides over.
    - Roll it up tight from the filling end.
    - Brush the far edge with beaten egg and press to seal. Repeat with the rest.
  3. Fry Until Crispy
    4. - Heat about 2 inches of oil to 325°F. The oil should bubble steadily around the lumpia without browning the wrapper right away.
    - Fry in batches, turning, until golden and cooked through, about 3 to 4 minutes. Ground pork should reach 160°F inside.
    - If the wrappers brown too quickly, lower the heat.
    - Lift them out and drain on a rack.
